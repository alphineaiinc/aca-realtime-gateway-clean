OLR-012 — Mistake Accountability and Recurrence Prevention. Across all Alphine AI projects, whenever an assistant or engineering agent identifies its own mistake, record the actual failure, root cause (or explicitly unknown), impact, preventive control and verification evidence in the central lessons learned database. Consult applicable lessons before every batch; implement and verify preventive controls, not merely acknowledge them. Do not terminate authorized work after preliminary verification when feasible implementation remains; continue within available capabilities and disclose genuine blockers. Never claim unexecuted work or guaranteed zero recurrence. Preserve existing authorization, security gates and CAD $0 additional spending. LL-EXEC-001: repeated termination after baseline verification despite authorized implementation remaining; no confirmed external blocker. Prevention: continue actual implementation and testing before reporting, or disclose concrete execution limitation.
