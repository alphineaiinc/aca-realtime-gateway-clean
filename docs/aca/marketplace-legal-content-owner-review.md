## Proposed marketplace privacy disclosure
Alphine ACA's proposed marketplace integration provides authenticated read-only access to the business profile associated with the connected ACA account. The implemented profile tool returns the business name, configured preferred language and region. It uses a verified account-to-tenant association to limit access to the connected business. It does not expose customer records, call recordings, call history, payments or profile modification through this tool. Authentication and security controls are used to restrict access.

## Proposed marketplace terms summary
The proposed marketplace integration allows an authorized ACA account holder to view the connected business profile. Users must be authorized to access the account and must not attempt to access another tenant, bypass security or misuse the service. The currently implemented marketplace tool is read-only and does not book appointments, modify records, process payments or provide customer call history. Any separate ACA subscription, voice functionality or commercial service is governed by the applicable agreed service terms.
