# Protected installation checklist — proposal only

- Owner selects separate trust administrator and verifies separation from candidate authors and engineering agents.
- Inventory effective repository/App permissions, protected workflow ownership, signer artifact and independent key pin. Do not use candidate-controlled configuration.
- Review exact immutable harness and tests, independently verify image provenance and platform manifest; record run/attempt and artifact identities.
- Confirm CAD 0 incremental cost and obtain explicit installation authorization before changing settings or provisioning.
- Dedicated ephemeral runner only; deny on ambiguous create, failed removal/query, residuals or cancellation. Cleanup step is best effort, not proof of daemon quiescence. Require independently evidenced runner destruction or quarantine before resolving ambiguous lifecycle incidents.
- Test fixed benign runtime, distinct fault denials, resource enforcement, and recovery evidence under separately authorized scope.
- Validate substantive disposition evidence and signer custody independently; no approval-only signing. Test deployed restricted database roles and protected ingress.
- Record independent exact-head clearance, protected acceptance and owner activation decision separately. No activation by this checklist.
